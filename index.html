    FAQ

    Questions agency owners ask

    Still unsure? Book a call and we'll walk through your account.

    Will my clients know you're a separate company?

    No. We reply under your agency name, from your support inbox and chat widget, following a playbook we write together.

    How do you access our HighLevel account?

    You add our team as agency users and choose their permissions. You can remove access at any time, and every action is visible in your audit logs.

    Are you affiliated with HighLevel?

    No. We're an independent team working inside your agency account. HighLevel's own support still covers platform outages and your agency billing.

    Can you fix A2P registrations that were already rejected?

    Yes. Most rejections come from mismatched opt-in language, missing privacy policy terms or vague use-case descriptions. We fix the forms and site copy, then resubmit.

    Can I order a service without a subscription?

    Yes. Every service is available on-demand as a custom request. On-demand rates are higher than plan rates, and subscribers get most services included, so a plan is the better value if you need help every month.

    What if I go over my sub-account limit?

    We keep supporting every account that month and let you know. If you stay over, we'll suggest the next plan. No surprise overage fees.

    Is there a contract?

    Monthly plans are month to month with 30 days' notice. Annual plans are paid up front and include two months free.
